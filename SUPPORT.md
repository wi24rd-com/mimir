# Support

Mimir is maintained by one person in spare time. There is no promised response
time and no individual support.

- **How do I ...?** The [README](README.md) covers setup, updating, the
  dashboard, badges and the limits. `node src/setup.mjs` can be run again and
  picks up where it stopped.
- **Collection has stopped.** The dashboard and `node src/show.mjs` say why when
  they can. The Actions tab of your own copy shows each run, and
  `gh run view --log-failed` shows what went wrong.
- **Something is broken in Mimir itself.** Open an issue in
  [wi24rd-com/mimir](https://github.com/wi24rd-com/mimir/issues) with the bug form.
- **A security problem.** Report it privately; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Your figures live in your own copy. Nobody at Wi24rd can see them or restore them.
